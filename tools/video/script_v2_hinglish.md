# RakshakPay – full video v2 (Hinglish) – script and storyboard

Horizontal 1920×1080, about 9 minutes. Base: the documentary style (slides + real app screens).
Cartoon characters for the acted parts. Captions in Roman Hinglish. No music.
Voices: AI4Bharat Indic Parler-TTS (natural, with emotion per line).

**Characters:** Priya (victim) • Pankaj (scammer) • Pavan (mule, Deoghar) • Pradeep (mule, Ranchi)
• Prem (last mule, buys a phone) • Mahesh (shopkeeper) • **Ravi (new: an ordinary customer)**
• Lakshmi (Kaveri Bank branch officer) • Inspector Meera (cyber police) • Narrator (voice only)

---

## 1. Hook (0:00–0:15) — characters
*Visual:* Priya's balance drops from ₹1,00,000 to ₹50,000; her shocked face.
- **NARRATOR:** Sirf do minute. Ek phone call. Aur pachaas hazaar rupaye gayab.
- **NARRATOR:** Yeh kahani Priya ki hai — aur kal yeh aapke kisi apne ki bhi ho sakti hai.

- **PANKAJ:** Hello, Priya ji? Main Kaveri Bank ke KYC department se baat kar raha hoon.
- **PRIYA:** Haan ji, boliye?
- **PANKAJ:** Aapka KYC expire ho chuka hai. Aaj update nahi hua, toh aapka account block ho jayega.
- **PRIYA** *(worried)*: Arre! Toh ab main kya karoon?
- **PANKAJ:** Ghabraiye mat. Bas pachaas hazaar ka ek verification payment kijiye. Kaam hote hi poori raqam wapas aa jayegi.
- **PRIYA:** Theek hai… bhej diya. *(real bank screen: −₹50,000)*
- **PANKAJ** *(sly)*: Bahut shukriya, Priya ji.

- **NARRATOR:** Inhe kehte hain *mule accounts* — aise log jo kuch paison ke liye apna bank account dusron ko istemaal karne dete hain.
- **NARRATOR:** Kuch hi minuton mein paisa saat accounts aur chaar bankon mein bikhar jaata hai.
- *Visual:* Deoghar — **Pavan at an ATM**, cash comes out.
- **NARRATOR:** Deoghar mein Pavan ATM se paanch hazaar nakad nikaal leta hai.
- **NARRATOR:** Das hazaar crypto mein badle gaye, aur unka ek hissa desh se bahar bhej diya gaya.
- *Visual:* Jaipur shop — Prem buys a phone from Mahesh.
- **PREM:** Bhaiya, woh naya phone dikhana. Das hazaar ka hai na? UPI se de deta hoon.
- **MAHESH:** Yeh lijiye sir. Payment aa gaya — dhanyavaad!

## 4. How it goes today (1:55–3:05) — slides + characters

## 6. The same fraud, with RakshakPay (3:30–7:20) — real app screens + characters

**6a. Complaint** — *real screens:* passbook → "Report this payment as fraud (1930)" → form → acknowledgement.
- **PRIYA:** Is payment ko main fraud report karti hoon.
- **NARRATOR:** Priya app mein hi woh payment chunti hai aur shikayat bhejti hai. Use turant ek acknowledgement number milta hai — jaanch ki baaki jaankari sirf police ke paas rehti hai.

**6b. Instant trace** — *real:* police dashboard, money trail, map replay.
- **MEERA:** Shikayat aate hi system ne poora paisa trace kar liya — har account, har bank.
- **NARRATOR:** System fraud ke baad ka har transaction samay ke kram mein dobara chalata hai, aur Priya ke paise ka peecha karta hai.

**6c. Hold only the victim's money** — *real:* complaint summary.
- **MEERA:** Pachchees hazaar mule accounts mein hold, aur lagbhag paanch hazaar ka crypto exchange par freeze.
- **NARRATOR:** Poora account band nahi hota — sirf utni raqam roki jaati hai jitni Priya ki us account tak pahunchi.

**6d. ATM withdrawals** — *real:* ATM pin on the map, "ATM cash-outs" hotspot, recovery lien for Pavan; Pavan's card declined at the ATM.
- **NARRATOR:** Jo paisa ATM se nikal chuka hai, uska bhi hisaab hai. Har ATM withdrawal ki jagah map par dikhti hai, aur zyada cash-out wale ilaake hotspot ban jaate hain.
- **NARRATOR:** Pavan ke nikaale paanch hazaar ke liye court application apne aap taiyaar hoti hai.
- **PAVAN** *(at the ATM)*: Yeh kya? Card hi nahi chal raha!
- **NARRATOR:** Mule list mein aate hi Pavan ka ATM card, UPI aur transfer — sab band.

**6e. Mules blocked on UPI** — *real:* Pradeep's RakshakPay app: "Your account is on the mule list – payments are blocked"; payment refused.
- **PRADEEP:** UPI se bhej deta hoon… arre, yeh bhi block!
- **NARRATOR:** UPI app par bhi mule ko saaf sandesh milta hai — payment band, branch jaakar verification karaiye. Haan, account mein aane wala paisa aata rahega, aur victim ka nuksaan bharega.

**6f. An ordinary person paying a mule (NEW: Ravi)** — *characters + real warning screens.*
- **RAVI:** Online ek purana phone dekha tha, saste mein mil raha hai. Bechne wale ko das hazaar bhej deta hoon.
- *Visual:* the red mule warning; then the "Pay anyway (repayment)" dialog.
- **RAVI** *(shocked)*: Yeh account mule list mein hai? Nahi bhai, payment cancel!
- **NARRATOR:** RakshakPay har us vyakti ko pehle hi chetavni deta hai jo mule account mein paisa bhejne ja raha ho. Ravi ka paisa bach gaya.
- **NARRATOR:** "Pay anyway" sirf us vyakti ke liye hai jo victim ka paisa lautana chahta hai — aisa paisa seedha victim ki recovery mein jaata hai.

**6g. PAN blacklist** — *characters:* Pankaj at a bank counter.
- **PANKAJ:** Koi baat nahi, naya account khol leta hoon…
- **NARRATOR:** Ab yeh mumkin nahi. Uska PAN har bank aur crypto exchange mein blacklist ho chuka hai.

**6h. The shopkeeper is protected** — *characters:* Mahesh "ACTIVE", Prem "−₹10,000".
- **MAHESH** *(relieved)*: Mera account bilkul theek chal raha hai!
- **NARRATOR:** Mahesh ne imaandaari se saamaan becha tha, isliye RakshakPay uske account ko haath bhi nahi lagata.
- **NARRATOR:** Woh das hazaar Prem se wasool hote hain — jisne fraud ke paise se phone khareeda tha. Court ki manzoori ke baad uske account par recovery lien lagta hai, chahe balance minus mein kyun na chala jaaye.
- **PREM:** Mera balance minus mein?!

**6i. Court applications, ready-made** — *real:* court orders list and the drafted application.
- **MEERA:** Court ke liye saari applications bhi system ne khud taiyaar kar di hain — account, raqam aur transaction number ke saath.

**6j. Location on every payment** — *real:* "Location is mandatory" popup.
- **NARRATOR:** Har payment ke saath phone ki location darj hoti hai. Location band? Toh payment bhi nahi.

**6k. Getting off the mule list (delisting)** — *characters:* Pavan at his branch with officer Lakshmi; *real:* officer's re-KYC, OTP, statement, recommendation; police decision; Pavan's notices.
- **PAVAN:** Madam, mujhe ek part-time job ke naam par phasaaya gaya tha. Mujhe nahi pata tha yeh fraud ka paisa hai.
- **LAKSHMI:** Original PAN, Aadhaar aur photo dikhaiye. Aapke mobile par OTP bhi bhej rahi hoon.
- **NARRATOR:** Mule list se bahar aane ka ek hi raasta hai — apni home branch jaakar asli documents ke saath dobara KYC.
- **NARRATOR:** Bank officer customer ka bayaan likhta hai aur apni salaah police ko bhejta hai. Aakhri faisla cyber police ka hota hai.
- **MEERA:** Pavan ko dhokha diya gaya tha. Services wapas chalu — lekin recovery jaari rahegi.
- **NARRATOR:** Teen faisle ho sakte hain. Begunaah — account hack hua ya documents ka galat istemaal hua: poori tarah list se bahar. Dhokhe ka shikaar — services wapas, lekin court wali recovery chukaani hogi. Aur jaan-boojhkar shaamil — account har bank mein band rehta hai, aur police case chalta hai.
- *Visual:* Pavan's app: Active, but −₹5,000 recovery lien.

**6l. Money back** — *real:* release, Priya's "Money returned" notice; characters cheer.
- **MEERA:** Court ki manzoori mil gayi hai. Raqam release kar rahi hoon.
- **PRIYA** *(joyful)*: Mera paisa wapas aa gaya!
- **NARRATOR:** Lagbhag tees hazaar rupaye seedhe Priya ke account mein — aur baaki raqam recovery lien se wasool hoti rahegi.

## 7. Stay safe: rules for every citizen (7:20–8:10) — slides + Priya and Ravi
- **NARRATOR:** Aur ab, aapki suraksha ke kuch zaroori niyam.
- **NARRATOR:** Ek. Koi bhi bank ya police phone par KYC update ya paise nahi maangti.
- **NARRATOR:** Do. OTP, UPI PIN ya CVV kisi ko mat bataiye. Paisa *lene* ke liye kabhi PIN daalna nahi padta.
- **NARRATOR:** Teen. Apna bank account, ATM card ya SIM kisi ko kiraye par mat dijiye. "Ghar baithe paisa ghumao" wali naukri, mule banne ka jaal hai.
- **NARRATOR:** Chaar. Payment se pehle naam aur chetavni dhyaan se padhiye. Mule warning dikhe toh payment rok dijiye.
- **NARRATOR:** Paanch. Fraud ho jaaye toh turant 1930 par call kijiye ya cybercrime.gov.in par shikayat kijiye — jitni jaldi, utna zyada paisa bachta hai.
- **NARRATOR:** Aur chhe. Phone ki location aur bank ke SMS alert hamesha chalu rakhiye.

## 8. The nine rules of RakshakPay (8:10–8:40) — slide grid
- **NARRATOR:** RakshakPay ke nau niyam: turant trace. Sirf victim ka paisa hold. Mule accounts par nishaan. Bhejne wale ko chetavni. Dukaandaar surakshit. PAN blacklist. Court ki manzoori se recovery. Crypto freeze. Aur har payment par location.

## 9. How we built it, and what's next (8:40–9:10) — slides
- **NARRATOR:** Humne nau Android apps Flutter mein banaye, server Python aur FastAPI par, aur database PostgreSQL par. Har app English, Hindi aur Kannada mein chalta hai.
- **NARRATOR:** Abhi yeh demo bankon par chalta hai. Asli duniya mein ise RBI, NPCI, bankon aur adalaton se jodna hoga. Aage hum fake shikayaton ko pehchaanne aur mule hotspots ka anumaan lagane par kaam karenge.

## 10. Ending (9:10–9:20) — characters
- **NARRATOR:** RakshakPay — har rupaye ki raksha.
