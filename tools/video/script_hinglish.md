# RakshakPay – story video (Hinglish) – script and storyboard

Horizontal 1920×1080, about 7–8 minutes. Flat 2D cartoon characters, real app screens inside the story.
Captions in Roman Hinglish. No music. Each character has their own voice.

**Voices:** Narrator (male, Madhur) • Priya (female, Swara) • Pankaj, scammer (male, deeper and faster)
• Inspector Meera (female, lower and firm) • Mahesh, shopkeeper (male, older, slower) • Prem, last mule (male, younger)

---

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:15)
*Visual:* black screen → Priya's phone: balance ₹1,00,000 drops to ₹50,000 with a red flash → Priya's shocked face.

- **NARRATOR:** Sirf do minute. Ek phone call. Aur pachaas hazaar rupaye gayab.
- **NARRATOR:** Yeh kahani Priya ki hai — aur kal yeh aapke kisi apne ki bhi ho sakti hai.

## Scene 2 — The scam call (0:15–1:00)
*Visual:* split screen. Left: Priya at home in Bengaluru, phone ringing. Right: Pankaj in a dark room in Jamtara, headset, laptop, a pile of SIM phones.

- **PANKAJ:** Hello, Priya ji? Main Kaveri Bank ke KYC department se baat kar raha hoon.
- **PRIYA:** Haan ji, boliye?
- **PANKAJ:** Aapka KYC expire ho chuka hai. Aaj update nahi hua, toh aapka account block ho jayega.
- **PRIYA** *(worried)*: Arre! Toh ab main kya karoon?
- **PANKAJ:** Ghabraiye mat. Bas pachaas hazaar ka ek verification payment kijiye. Kaam hote hi poori raqam wapas aa jayegi.
- *Visual:* close-up of Priya's real bank app screen: −₹50,000 to Pankaj.
- **PRIYA:** Theek hai… bhej diya.
- **PANKAJ** *(smiles slyly)*: Bahut shukriya, Priya ji. *(call cuts)*

## Scene 3 — The money runs (1:00–1:45)
*Visual:* India map. Coins fly from Bengaluru to Jamtara, then split to Kolkata and Bharatpur, then Deoghar, Nuh, Ranchi and Jaipur. A mule character pops up at each city.

- **NARRATOR:** Paisa milte hi Pankaj use turant aage bhej deta hai — ek account se doosre, doosre se teesre.
- **NARRATOR:** Inhe kehte hain *mule accounts* — aise log jo kuch paison ke liye apna bank account dusron ko istemaal karne dete hain.
- **NARRATOR:** Kuch hi minuton mein paisa saat accounts aur chaar bankon mein bikhar jaata hai.
- *Visual:* Ranchi — coins turn into a crypto coin and fly off-screen.
- **NARRATOR:** Das hazaar crypto mein badle gaye, aur unka ek hissa desh se bahar bhej diya gaya.
- *Visual:* Jaipur — Prem walks into Mahesh's mobile shop.
- **PREM:** Bhaiya, woh naya phone dikhana. Das hazaar ka hai na? UPI se de deta hoon.
- **MAHESH** *(happy)*: Yeh lijiye sir. Payment aa gaya — dhanyavaad!

## Scene 4 — How it goes today (1:45–3:00)
*Visual:* Priya gets the debit SMS, realises, calls 1930.

- **PRIYA** *(crying)*: Hello, 1930? Mere saath fraud ho gaya hai… pachaas hazaar!
- **NARRATOR:** Priya turant shikayat karti hai. Lekin aaj ka tareeka bahut dheema hai.
- *Visual:* police office, paper stack, an officer typing letters; calendar flips Day 1 → Day 3 → Day 7.
- **NARRATOR:** Shikayat ek ticket ban jaati hai. Phir har bank ko alag-alag letter jaata hai — "yeh paisa kahan gaya?"
- **NARRATOR:** Jab tak jawab aata hai, mule accounts khaali ho chuke hote hain.

**The shopkeeper's loss (today's system)**
*Visual:* Mahesh's shop; his bank app shows a big red "ACCOUNT FROZEN" stamp; Mahesh shocked.

- **NARRATOR:** Aur is poori kahani mein sabse bada nuksaan hota hai ek imaandaar dukaandaar ka.
- **MAHESH:** Maine toh phone imaandaari se becha tha! Mera poora account kyun band kar diya?
- **NARRATOR:** Fraud ka paisa jis bhi account tak pahunchta hai, use aksar poora freeze kar diya jaata hai — dukaandaar ka bhi. Mahesh ka phone bhi gaya, aur uski kamaai bhi atak gayi.
- **NARRATOR:** Aur Priya? Use apna paisa wapas paane ke liye court ke order ka mahinon intezaar karna padta hai.

## Scene 5 — Enter RakshakPay (3:00–3:30)
*Visual:* logo reveal; connected system: 5 banks, RakshakPay UPI, Business sound box, CoinVault, Cyber Police — all linked to one server.

- **NARRATOR:** Isi samasya ka hal hai — RakshakPay.
- **NARRATOR:** Paanch bank, UPI app, dukaan ka sound box, crypto exchange aur cyber police — sab ek hi system se jude hue.
- **NARRATOR:** Ab wahi kahani dobara dekhiye — is baar RakshakPay ke saath.

## Scene 6 — The same fraud, with RakshakPay (3:30–6:00)

**6a. Complaint** — *Visual:* Priya opens her bank app → "Report fraud (1930)"; real screen of her complaint.
- **PRIYA:** Is payment ko main fraud report karti hoon.
- **NARRATOR:** Shikayat darj hote hi Priya ko ek acknowledgement number milta hai. Jaanch ki baaki jaankari sirf police ke paas rehti hai.

**6b. Instant trace** — *Visual:* Inspector Meera at her desk; the monitor shows the real police dashboard, then the money moving on the map.
- **MEERA:** Shikayat aate hi system ne poora paisa trace kar liya hai — har account, har bank.
- **NARRATOR:** System fraud ke baad ka har transaction samay ke kram mein dobara chalata hai, aur Priya ke paise ka peecha karta hai.

**6c. Hold only the victim's money** — *Visual:* real complaint summary.
- **MEERA:** Tees hazaar mule accounts mein hold ho gaye, aur paanch hazaar ka crypto exchange par freeze.
- **NARRATOR:** Dhyaan dijiye — poora account band nahi hota. Sirf utni raqam roki jaati hai jitni Priya ki us account tak pahunchi.

**6d. Mules marked** — *Visual:* Pankaj tries to send money and gets blocked; then a neighbour trying to pay a mule sees the real red warning.
- **PANKAJ** *(frustrated)*: Yeh kya? Mera account toh chal hi nahi raha!
- **NARRATOR:** Saare mule accounts *mule list* mein aa jaate hain. Jab tak branch jaakar verification na ho, unse paisa nahi nikal sakta.
- **NARRATOR:** Aur koi in accounts mein paisa bhejne lage, toh use pehle hi chetavni mil jaati hai.

**6e. PAN blacklist** — *Visual:* Pankaj at a bank counter and on the CoinVault app; both show "PAN blacklisted".
- **PANKAJ:** Koi baat nahi, naya account khol leta hoon…
- **NARRATOR:** Ab yeh mumkin nahi. Uska PAN har bank aur crypto exchange mein blacklist ho chuka hai.

**6f. The shopkeeper is protected** — *Visual:* Mahesh's app shows "Active" with a green tick; Prem's account shows −₹10,000 (negative balance).
- **MAHESH** *(relieved)*: Mera account bilkul theek chal raha hai!
- **NARRATOR:** Mahesh ne imaandaari se saamaan becha tha, isliye RakshakPay uske account ko haath bhi nahi lagata.
- **NARRATOR:** Woh das hazaar Prem se wasool hote hain — jisne fraud ke paise se phone khareeda tha. Court ki manzoori ke baad uske account par *recovery lien* lagta hai, chahe balance minus mein kyun na chala jaaye.
- **PREM** *(shocked)*: Mera balance minus mein?!
- **NARRATOR:** Ab Prem ke account mein jo bhi paisa aayega, sabse pehle Priya ka nuksaan bharega.

**6g. Court application, ready-made** — *Visual:* real court application screen on Meera's monitor.
- **MEERA:** Court ke liye application bhi system ne khud taiyaar kar di hai — saare account, raqam aur transaction number ke saath.

**6h. Location on every payment** — *Visual:* real "Location is mandatory" popup.
- **NARRATOR:** Har payment ke saath phone ki location bhi darj hoti hai. Location band? Toh payment bhi nahi.

**6i. Money back** — *Visual:* Meera clicks Release; Priya's phone buzzes; real "Money returned" notice; Priya cheers.
- **MEERA:** Court ki manzoori mil gayi hai. Raqam release kar rahi hoon.
- **PRIYA** *(joyful)*: Mera paisa wapas aa gaya!
- **NARRATOR:** Lagbhag paintees hazaar rupaye seedhe Priya ke account mein — aur baaki raqam recovery lien se wasool hoti rahegi.

## Scene 7 — The nine rules, quick recap (6:00–6:50)
*Visual:* 9 icons appear one by one in a grid.

- **NARRATOR:** RakshakPay ke nau niyam: turant trace. Sirf victim ka paisa hold. Mule accounts par nishaan. Paisa bhejne wale ko chetavni. Dukaandaar surakshit. PAN blacklist. Court ki manzoori se recovery. Crypto freeze. Aur har payment par location.

## Scene 8 — How we built it, and what's next (6:50–7:30)
- **NARRATOR:** Humne nau Android apps Flutter mein banaye, server Python aur FastAPI par, aur database PostgreSQL par. Har app English, Hindi aur Kannada mein chalta hai.
- **NARRATOR:** Abhi yeh demo bankon par chalta hai. Asli duniya mein ise RBI, NPCI, bankon aur adalaton se jodna hoga. Aage hum fake shikayaton ko pehchaanne aur mule hotspots ka anumaan lagane par kaam karenge.

## Scene 9 — Ending (7:30–7:45)
*Visual:* Priya, Meera and Mahesh smiling and waving; RakshakPay logo.

- **NARRATOR:** RakshakPay — har rupaye ki raksha.
